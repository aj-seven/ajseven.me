### Figuring out
As far, I continue about that for couple of days. Eventually, I figured out about pinout configurations of transistor which is more matters in building like follows as **CBE (collector, base, emitter)**. I tried reading numbers on the transistor that collected from boards that checked more about it. I come to know that every comp has its own datasheet which follows different pinouts in the transistor based on numbers (ha exhausting part to me). I have keypad mobile type at that time :).

Soon, based on that info - I again tried :) but I never got that. Then there is resistors and capacitor part again those values matters as well in building electronics circuits. So, I tried searched online about it like capacitor values and resistor values based on color codes. I tried to figure out by looking colors from online without much complexity of calculation (I hate that, eventually it needed later). Then, again I tried with the resistors and capacitor values in circuit I followed and **boom** it works :).

That small sound from 3mm jack to speaker got large sound and work like sweet and I felt like achieved something great.

And the main part comes - that achievement felt troublesome later :( I noticed that transistor got heating up, I thought it is normal part under working of circuit. But, it says nope as I keep work of that circuit played some more songs eventually I soldered pins getting desoldered itself :( due to that I stopped that and removed the battery.

As search continues, what could be the reason for this?? I searched online that heating of transistor - I came to know that it need to placed in metal type like aluminum. Known as heat sink which dissipate heat :).

### Finally it works!!
So, I searched in boards to find that heat sink :) and put it on the transistor with screwed and again tried to play the music :) boom it works. it heats normally, and continue to work even after 1 hour :)
